Our Story

A Legacy Born in a Kitchen

On The Rocks began as a dream — to bring the warm, soul-nourishing flavours of South Indian home cooking to every table. Founded in 2004 by Meenakshi Sundar and her family in Chennai, our restaurant has grown from a modest tiffin centre into a beloved destination for food lovers across the city.

Every dish we serve carries the memory of a grandmother's recipe, the precision of a seasoned cook, and the love of a family that believes food is the most sincere form of hospitality.

We source our spices directly from farmers in Chettinad and the Nilgiris, grind our masalas fresh each morning, and cook every meal to order. No shortcuts. Just tradition, taste, and heart.
